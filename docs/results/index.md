# Results

Dated measurements.

* [LB 686 at 40.8 Gbps into sc-demo with two receivers, stopped at 10 TB (2026-11-12 20:37:25–21:10:35 UTC)](2026-11-12-203725UTC-lb686-40gbps-10tb.md) - Run e2sar_lb686_1794515844: the external senders on LB 686 streamed 26.5M events (~381 KB, 40.8 Gbps, twice the morning's rate) for 33 min to e2sar_daos_recv on ebpf2201 and ebpf2203 (32 receive threads, 16 DAOS threads each), no reader; stopped at 10.10 TB (9.19 TiB) in sc-demo. 0 DAOS failures, 0 enqueue loss, 29,008 events lost in reassembly (1.09×10⁻³, 40× the 20.9 Gbps run). 79% NIC port discards, 99% of them on ebpf2201 (376,850 packets against 776 on ebpf2203 at the same load), 21% upstream, 0.6% kernel IP→UDP drops. Socket buffers, NIC rings and softnet never dropped. Also: a 7 min 45 Gbps run with 32 DAOS threads (6.1×10⁻⁴) and a 1 min run with the receive threads pinned to NUMA 0, which made NIC port discards ~10× worse.
* [LB 686 into sc-demo with two receivers, stopped at 10 TB (2026-11-12 04:53:34–05:57:43 UTC)](2026-11-12-045334UTC-lb686-two-workers-10tb.md) - Run e2sar_lb686_1794459212: an external sender streamed 58.8M events (~171 KB, 20.9 Gbps) for 64 min through LB 686 to e2sar_daos_recv on ebpf2201 and ebpf2203 (16 receive threads each), no reader; stopped at 10.06 TB (9.15 TiB) in the pool sc-demo. 0 DAOS failures, 0 enqueue loss, 1,619 events lost in reassembly (2.75×10⁻⁵). Each loss matched with the node's 1 s NIC and kernel counters: 58% NIC port discards (rx_discards_phy), 36% upstream (no counter change on either node while both lost), 5% packets the kernel took in at IP but never handed to UDP, 6 events with no explanation. Socket buffers, NIC rings and softnet never dropped. New script scripts/e2sar_loss_timeline.py draws the loss timeline by cause.
* [LB 686 into sc-demo with a --live-rm reader on the other node (2026-11-12 00:46:30–01:24:01 UTC)](2026-11-12-004630UTC-lb686-live-rm.md) - Run e2sar_lb686_1794444385: an external sender streamed 34.2M events (~26 KB, 3.17 Gbps, 890.8 GB) for 37.5 min through LB 686 to e2sar_daos_recv on ebpf2201, into the new pool sc-demo; e2sar_daos_read --live-rm --interval 2 on ebpf2203 read everything back and emptied DAOS 28 min after the sender stopped. Receiver: 0 DAOS failures, 1 event lost in reassembly. 121,916 event numbers (0.355%) never arrived: one 1,136-event gap at 00:57:54 UTC matches 23,076 packets the kernel took in at IP but never counted as UDP on ebpf2201; the rest came in bursts no counter on ebpf2201 saw. The reader is the bottleneck (~1.8 Gbps): DAOS peaked at 354 GiB. Also: how big the container would be with --live.
* [Reading sc26 from 16 Slurm TB nodes with e2sar_daos_read --fetch (2026-11-11)](2026-11-11-tb-nodes-fetch.md) - e2sar_daos_read --fetch on the Slurm TB nodes, reading LB 677 run 1 (161 GB, ~9.5 KB events) from sc26. One reader process is CPU-bound on one thread at 1.2–1.3 Gbps on a tb16 node, 1.6 Gbps on tb18c07 and 1.8 Gbps on tblogin1902; 16 nodes with one reader each read 14.8 Gbps together (sum of per-node rates), half of them waiting on DAOS. A run can't be split between readers. Right after a 128-reader test (8 per node) every data read in sc26 timed out on engine rank 4 (daosfs2302), still at 21:45 UTC. Lists the bugs, failures and proposals.
* [LB 682 rate sweep, with and without a sender ramp (2026-11-10)](2026-11-10-lb682-rate-sweep-ramp.md) - One receiver on ebpf2201, ~100 KB EVIO events through LB 682 at 20, 15, 10 and 5 Gbps for 90 s each. Without a ramp the fastest lossless rate was 10 Gbps (15 Gbps lost 21 of 1.66M, 20 Gbps 101 of 2.21M), most of it in the first 10 s. With e2sar_evio_send --ramp 10 (1 Gbps up to the rate over 10 s) the start-up burst was gone: lossless up to 15 Gbps, and 20 Gbps lost 3. Every loss was a NIC port discard (rx_discards_phy).
* [End-to-end results: summary](e2e-summary.md) - The highest lossless rates node-to-node and through the LB, what loses events, what limits the rate, and latency, across the E2E runs of 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-28.
* [LB 682: Perlmutter EVIO time frames, receiver crashes and losses (2026-11-09)](2026-11-09-lb682-perlmutter-losses.md) - Eleven runs on LB 682 (19:21–22:22 UTC): EVIO time frames of 28–45 kB at 3.4–3.8 Gbps (~11k events/s). The DAOS receiver was never the sender's bottleneck (no back-pressure over UDP; 0 enqueue loss, 0 failed writes, ~1/5 of its tested capacity). Losses: 3 MB socket buffers (~10%, fixed with -b 1 GiB); from 20:33, receiver crashes in E2SAR's reassembly when two senders send different events with the same event number and data id (LUX and Perlmutter, then several Perlmutter frame builders); and 26–32% of event numbers that never arrive (a 65.536 µs frame counter, not all frames sent).
* [LB 677 run 2: EVIO time frames from NERSC, losses and stopping a receiver (2026-11-05 20:18:15–20:55:35 UTC)](2026-11-05-201815UTC-lb677-run2-evio.md) - Run 2 on LB 677 (e2sar_lb677_1793909535): 34.05M EVIO time frames of one ROC (~10 KB each, ~1.23 Gbps) in 37 min, 9,583 missing (0.028%) in three bursts: 584 on the way to ebpf2201 at 20:27:50 (the half-hourly pattern of run 1), 7,600 from stopping ebpf2201 mid-run (a shutdown-order bug in e2sar_daos_recv), and 1,399 at 20:41:30 on ebpf2203 alone, either lost inside it or never reached it (its data port also carries NFS and SSH). Zero NIC and socket-buffer drops on both nodes.
* [LB 677 run 1: EVIO time frames from NERSC to two receivers (2026-11-05 19:47:30–20:06:04 UTC)](2026-11-05-194730UTC-lb677-run1-evio.md) - Run 1 on LB 677 (e2sar_lb677_1793905825, 19:47:30 to 20:06:04 UTC): two senders streamed 17.0M EVIO time frames of one ROC (~9.5 KB each, 97.2% hit words, 1.16 Gbps) for 18.6 min through LB 677 on the beta control plane to e2sar_daos_recv on ebpf2201 and ebpf2203, read live with --live into /scratch/epsdaq: 536 events (0.003%) missing, all in one 76 ms burst that hit only ebpf2201's share, with zero NIC or socket drops on either node, so the events were lost before ebpf2201. Bursts recur at :27:50 and :57:50 UTC.
* [EVIO frames from NERSC through LB 675 to two receivers (2026-11-04)](2026-11-04-nersc-lb675-evio.md) - A NERSC sender streamed 46.9M EVIO time frames (~207 B each, 25 Mbit/s) for 51 min through LB 675 on the beta control plane to e2sar_daos_recv on ebpf2201 and ebpf2203, read live with --live: 1,690 events (0.0036%) missing, all in 3 bursts; NIC counters were not recorded on both nodes, so where they were lost is open.
* [20 KB EVIO events at 2.5 Gbps, with a --live-rm reader (2026-10-30)](2026-10-30-evio-20kb-2p5gbps.md) - e2sar_evio_send -f 50 (50 frames, ~20 KB per event), 4 streams at 2.5 Gbps node to node into e2sar_daos_recv, read live with --live-rm: lossless and byte-exact, but the reader writes a 2 s bundle in ~2.1 s and falls slowly behind.
* [Receiver tuning (2026-10-29)](2026-10-29-receiver-tuning.md) - The e2sar_daos_recv sweep of --threads, --deq, --inflight and --flush-us for small events, e2sar_ft files and 1 MiB events up to 80 Gbps, and the recommended settings.
* [Perlmutter → ESnet LB → two ebpf receivers into DAOS, 10–80 Gbps (2026-10-27)](2026-10-27-perlmutter-rate-sweep.md) - Five runs of 10⁶ events of 1 MiB from one Perlmutter compute node (e2sar_perf) through the ESnet LB to e2sar_daos_recv on ebpf2201 and ebpf2203, into DAOS (pool sc26, the old KV layout), at 10, 20, 40 and twice 80 Gbps aggregate. Both ~80 Gbps runs lost the fewest events: 17 (1.7×10⁻⁵) at 78.3 Gbps and 97 (9.7×10⁻⁵) at 79.8 Gbps, each in one burst ~15 s in. 40 Gbps lost 4,902 (0.49%) in steps every ~10 s for the whole run, with 22,531 / 14,227 NIC port discards; 20 and 10 Gbps lost 339 and 128 the same way. 0 enqueue loss, 0 failed DAOS writes, 0 socket buffer drops in every run; receive → commit p50 0.5–0.7 ms, p99 ~1 ms.
